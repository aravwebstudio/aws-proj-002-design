Why parents choose us

Every child is unique and full of potential.

At Aarambh Vidya Playschool, we believe that every child is unique and full of potential. Our aim is to provide a safe, joyful and stimulating environment where children can learn through play and exploration.

Our vision and mission

  • Playful and joyful learningPlay-based and activity-based, every single day.
  • Individual attentionSmall groups so each child is seen and heard.
  • Safe, secure and child-friendlyCCTV coverage, child-safe furniture and hygienic daycare.
  • Experienced and caring teachersWarm, patient and trained for the early years.
  • Moral values and good habitsKindness, sharing and routine built into the day.
  • English-speaking environmentConfident communication from the very start.
Programs

One step at a time, from 2 to 5

Each program builds on the last, so children move up feeling confident and ready.

  1. 2+ years

    Playgroup

    A gentle first step away from home. Short, playful sessions build comfort, routine and early social confidence.

  2. 3+ years

    Nursery

    Rhymes, stories, colours, shapes and lots of movement, with growing independence.

  3. 4+ years

    LKG

    Early literacy and numeracy through activities, alongside creativity, curiosity and good habits.

  4. 5 years

    UKG

    School-readiness: reading, writing and number foundations, and confident English speaking.

  5. All ages

    Daycare

    Safe, secure and nurturing care in the same familiar campus, beyond regular school hours.

About & vision

Foundation for a bright future

At Aarambh Vidya Playschool, we believe that every child is unique and full of potential. Our aim is to provide a safe, joyful and stimulating environment where children can learn through play and exploration.

Our vision

To nurture young minds with love, care and quality education.

Our mission
  • Child-centred learning
  • Encouraging creativity and curiosity
  • Building strong moral values
  • Developing social skills
How we teach

Experiential, play-based learning

Young children learn best by doing. Our days are built around activities: rhymes, stories, games, art and craft, music and dance. Each one is chosen to grow a different part of the child: cognitive, social, emotional, physical and creative.

Classes are small and teachers know every child by name, so attention is individual and progress is noticed and celebrated.
